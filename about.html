Join the waitlist How it works

Civic information is scattered

Public updates sit across dozens of sites, pages, and feeds. It is hard to find the ones that apply to your area.

  • Many sites

    Government pages, LGU sites, and advisories each live in their own place.

  • Many pages

    A notice can be posted once and then be difficult to find again.

  • Many feeds

    News and public social posts move quickly, and local items get buried.

How it works

Three steps we are building. This is the plan, not a live service yet.

  1. Gather

    Civi gathers public sources: government sites, LGU pages, public advisories, news, and public social posts.

  2. Organize

    Items are sorted by place and by topic: advisories, weather and disaster, roads and traffic, utilities and outages, public services, local news, and events.

  3. Follow

    You follow your area and get a clean feed of what those public sources said.

Who it's for

Civi is for anyone who wants public updates for a place in the Philippines.

  • Residents

    People who want public updates for the place they live.

  • Commuters

    People moving between places who need roads, traffic, and advisories.

  • OFW families

    Families abroad checking public updates from home.

  • Small businesses

    Owners watching weather, outages, and other public notices that affect a shop or route.

  • Journalists

    Reporters looking across public sources by place and topic.

  • Researchers

    People studying what public sources have said about an area.

Questions

Where does the info come from?

From public sources on the internet: government sites, LGU pages, public advisories, news, and public social posts. Civi does not use private accounts or private groups.

Is it official?

No. Civi aggregates public sources and is planned to link each item to the original. It is not an official government service.

Is it free?

Joining the waitlist is free. We have not set a price for Civi.

Which areas?

Coverage is planned across the Philippines: Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao. No area is live yet. The map above is a placeholder.
